Pricing 2026

Prices 2026, per person, for a standard beading tour.

  • Day trip £120
  • 2 day tour including 1 night £285
  • 3 day tour including 2 nights £400
  • 4 day tour including 3 nights £530
  • 5 day tour including 4 nights £670
  • 9-day / special tours please email

Please email for day-trip availability. Days can be shorter or longer depending on when you arrive. Other special beading tours are also available — please email for more info.

Prices include hotel, breakfast and transport. Flights are not included.

A possible 1-person supplement applies if you can’t find anyone to join you. Minimum 2 people; we can cater for any size group.

Accommodation

You get your own room as standard — private room with en-suite bathroom — unless you specifically ask to share with a friend or partner. There is no single-room supplement.

We stay in family-run pensions in the heart of Jablonec. Continental breakfast is included, and there is a rest area to chat and bead together when there is time.

Nights in Prague

Nights in Prague can be arranged; email us for details. (Minimum 2 people per tour.)
