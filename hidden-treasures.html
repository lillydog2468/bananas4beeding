Hidden treasures

Beads are the heart of every trip, but Czechia has many other hidden treasures — historic towns, remarkable churches and quiet old corners that are well worth a day out.

Český Krumlov, St Barbara’s Church in Kutná Hora, and a Gothic crypt.

Český Krumlov

A small town in South Bohemia, wrapped around a bend in the Vltava and watched over by its castle. Its historic centre is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Kutná Hora

Once a wealthy silver-mining town, Kutná Hora is known for St Barbara’s Church, a striking Gothic church, and its historic centre is also UNESCO-listed.
