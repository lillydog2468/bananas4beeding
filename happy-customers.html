Ilana Joselowitz May 2018, 2019

This tour is a must for all beaders and bead lovers! I had visited Jablonec nad Nisou to shop for beads in the past and it became clear to me that I needed an “insider” to take me off the beaten track to areas that I would not have otherwise discovered. Bananas4Beading tours with Keith as a guide provided me with that and so much more!

Keith picked up our group of four at Prague airport, drove us to Jablonec and our adventure began the next morning. He had structured a well-organized yet flexible itinerary for our four-day tour which included visits to wonderful shops, factories and museums which are nestled in the fairy-tale scenery around Jablonec.

Keith is a gold mine of information and conveys his in-depth knowledge and passion for beads with endless enthusiasm. This tour is not just about bead shopping; it also provides the opportunity to see how beads are manufactured and to learn about the fascinating history of the legendary glass-making and bead industry in the area.

Each location that we visited boasted its own specialty from the latest 2-hole beads with special finishing treatments to traditional pressed beads and buttons as well as lamp-work and table beads. I often felt that I was literally touching history when I discovered that most of these magnificent beads are still produced using centuries-old equipment and traditional techniques.

Undoubtedly one of the highlights of our tour was a visit to a shop which sells antique beads, buttons and findings. This was a floor-to ceiling emporium of drawers and boxes overflowing with vintage beads and other treasures, many of which were still in their original wrappings. (We could not tear ourselves away and could only be placated by Keith promising to bring us back for a return visit on the last day of our tour!)

Bead addicts return year after year for another tour lead by Keith and it is easy to understand why. The lure of the chase and the feeling that there will always be more hidden treasures just waiting to be found made us want to sign up for the next trip as soon as this one ended. And now we know that while we are in Keith’s capable hands we will definitely find those treasures!
